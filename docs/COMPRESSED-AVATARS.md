# Compressed avatars / Komprimierte Figuren / Avatars compressés / Avatares comprimidos

**English.** Imported GLB2, VRM0/1 and local glTF2 sources can use Draco geometry, EXT/KHR Meshopt buffers and BasisU KTX2 textures. All decoders ship with OrbitPal and work offline in Studio, native overlays and OBS. Local glTF imports embed their allowed relative BIN/PNG/JPEG/KTX2 files into one portable GLB. External URLs, escaping paths, symlinks and resource budgets remain checked. There is no decoder preference or calibration to configure.

## Compatibility and limits

The decoder files match the exact locked Three 0.186.1 release. KTX2 chooses a texture target from the actual renderer's capabilities and can fall back to RGBA. Native validation supports 2D ETC1S/BasisLZ and UASTC LDR, including Zstandard-wrapped UASTC. HDR, array/cubemap/3D textures and arbitrary GPU-native KTX formats are not accepted through this extension.

The 256 MiB model, 8 MiB metadata, 64 MiB image-file and existing URI/relative-file constraints remain. Meshopt ranges/strides/counts and declared Draco attributes/Uint32 indices share a 256 MiB decoded-data budget. KTX2 headers, level ranges and dimensions (up to 16384 pixels/edge and 64 megapixels) are checked before decoding, with a 256 MiB RGBA mip budget across all distinct texture images in a model. Both shipped Draco/Basis WASM memories have a reproducible 256 MiB maximum (down from upstream's 2 GiB), enforced by WebAssembly before allocation/growth; tests attempt growth beyond the maximum and verify the initial buffer does not grow. Draco additionally checks the real aligned attribute/Uint32-index output total across all primitives of each model before JavaScript copies, independently of untrusted accessor counts. Trusted per-load task tokens bind queued buffers to their model budget even before WASM initialization; the reservation occurs after bounded `wasm_malloc`, before JS output copies. The renderer rejects inconsistent actual Draco counts before presentation. These are per-codec/output budgets, not a promise about total process RSS or GPU-driver memory.

Worker errors, decode failures, 60-second timeouts and stage disposal explicitly reject pending callers and retire the codec generation before terminating workers. Late initialization cannot revive a worker, late scenes are disposed, and a retry creates fresh codecs. Missing texture/geometry data is surfaced instead of silently displaying an incomplete model.

See [decoder licenses/hashes and static Basis bindings](../public/decoders/README.md), [offline fixtures](../tests/fixtures/compressed-avatar/README.md), [OBS policy](OBS-SECURITY.md), and official Three [GLTFLoader](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/GLTFLoader.html), [DRACOLoader](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/DRACOLoader.html), [KTX2Loader](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/KTX2Loader.html) documentation. Local fixtures exercise Chrome with SwiftShader; live OBS/CEF, WKWebView and WebView2 still depend on their installed browser engines and platform graphics drivers.
